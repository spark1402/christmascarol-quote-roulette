How to use this for revision

Choose a theme, generate a quotation and write an answer before revealing the guidance. The GCSE-style prompts are thematic practice questions, not past-paper questions. For a full exam response, connect the extract or quotation to the novella as a whole.

What does a strong analytical paragraph do?

It makes a clear argument, selects precise evidence, analyses Dickens’s methods and their effects, links to his wider message, and integrates relevant context rather than bolting it on.

What do AO1, AO2 and AO3 mean?

AO1: informed, critical response supported by references. AO2: analysis of language, form and structure. AO3: understanding of relationships between text and context. Check your exam board’s current assessment details.
